1) Otevřít ve visual studiu View / Terminal
2) Spustit: keytool -genkey -v -keystore shadows.keystore -alias Key -keyalg RSA -keysize 2048 -validity 10000
3) Zadat heslo: shadowspwd a další informace
4) Do maui projektu pridat nastaveni

	<PropertyGroup Condition="$(TargetFramework.Contains('-android')) and '$(Configuration)' == 'Release'">
		<AndroidKeyStore>True</AndroidKeyStore>
		<AndroidSigningKeyStore>..\shadows.keystore</AndroidSigningKeyStore>
		<AndroidSigningKeyAlias>Key</AndroidSigningKeyAlias>
		<AndroidSigningKeyPass>shadowspwd</AndroidSigningKeyPass>
		<AndroidSigningStorePass>shadowspwd</AndroidSigningStorePass>
	</PropertyGroup>
